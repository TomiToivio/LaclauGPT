# LaclauGPT reading suggestions

This is a cumulative list of literature that appears directly relevant to LaclauGPT but has not necessarily been added as a local PDF source yet.

Use this file for **reading candidates / related work discovered during research**. When a PDF is later added to `sources/pdfs/` and actually processed, add or update the corresponding entry in `SOURCES.md` and, when useful, `SUMMARIES.md`.

Agents should append newly discovered LaclauGPT-related books and papers here whenever they encounter them during repository work, literature searches, issue preparation, or research discussions. Prefer verified bibliographic metadata, update existing entries rather than duplicating them, and keep the list curated rather than exhaustive.

## Closest conceptual precedents: Laclau, empty/floating signifiers, AI and digital discourse

### Elanor Colleoni, Laura Illia & Stelios Zyglidopoulos — *Exploring How Publics Discursively Organize as Digital Collectives: The Use of Empty and Floating Signifiers as Organizing Devices in Social Media* (2021)

- **Venue:** Journal of the Association for Consumer Research 6(4), 491–502
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1086/716067
- **Topics:** Laclau; empty signifiers; floating signifiers; social media; digital collectives; publics
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** One of the closest precedents for applying Laclau's empty/floating-signifier concepts directly to social-media publics. Useful for theorizing how heterogeneous actors aggregate around open signifiers.

### Michael Strange & Jason Tucker — *Global governance and the normalization of artificial intelligence as ‘good’ for human health* (2024)

- **Venue:** AI & Society 39, 2667–2676
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-023-01774-2
- **Topics:** AI discourse; Laclau; empty signifier; global governance; crisis; re-articulation
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Very close to the AI26 substantive question. Explicitly argues that “artificial intelligence” can function as a Laclaudian empty signifier and traces institutional re-articulation across WHO, UNESCO and OHCHR.

### Eugenia Stamboliev & Tim Christiaens — *How empty is Trustworthy AI? A discourse analysis of the Ethics Guidelines of Trustworthy AI* (2024)

- **Venue:** Critical Policy Studies
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1080/19460171.2024.2315431
- **Topics:** Trustworthy AI; Laclau; empty signifier; hegemony; chains of equivalence; AI policy; critical discourse analysis
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Directly operationalizes Laclau's empty signifier, hegemony and chains of equivalence in AI policy discourse. Especially relevant for AI governance and elite/institutional arenas.

### Alberto Romele — *“Éthique de l’intelligence artificielle” comme signifiant flottant: considérations théoriques et analyse critique des discours de presse* / *“Ethics of artificial intelligence” as a floating signifier* (2024)

- **Venue:** Interfaces numériques
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.25965/interfaces-numeriques.5229
- **Topics:** Laclau; floating signifier; AI ethics; press discourse; discourse analysis
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Direct precedent for treating an AI-related concept as a floating signifier and studying how competing actors articulate it in media discourse.

### Alberto Romele — *|Ethics of AI| as a Floating Signifier; or Towards a Politics of AI Ethics* (2025)

- **In:** Rosa Fioravante & Antonino Vaccaro (eds.), *Humanism and Artificial Intelligence*, Springer Nature Switzerland, pp. 157–168
- **Topics:** AI ethics; floating signifier; Laclau; politics of AI
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** A compact theoretical bridge between Laclau's floating-signifier concept and contemporary AI ethics discourse.

### Veronica Barassi — *Contested visions: Digital discourses as empty signifiers from the ‘network’ to ‘big data’* (2016)

- **Venue:** Communication and the Public 1(4), 423–435
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1177/2057047316680220
- **Topics:** empty signifiers; floating signifiers; network; big data; techno-utopianism; techno-dystopianism; hegemonic struggle
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Strong historical predecessor for the argument that successive technology terms become contested signifiers through which political visions struggle for hegemony. Useful for positioning AI/AGI in a longer genealogy.

## AGI discourse and ideology

### Ross Graham — *Discourse analysis of academic debate of ethics for AGI* (2022)

- **Venue:** AI & Society 37, 1519–1532
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-021-01228-7
- **Topics:** AGI; existential risk; ethics; discourse analysis; academic communities
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Directly studies AGI discourse and differences between intellectual communities discussing AGI ethics. Useful empirical comparison for AI26.

### Borhane Blili-Hamelin, Leif Hancox-Li & Andrew Smart — *Unsocial Intelligence: An Investigation of the Assumptions of AGI Discourse* (2024)

- **Venue:** Proceedings of the AAAI/ACM Conference on AI, Ethics, and Society 7(1), 141–155
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1609/aies.v7i1.31625
- **Topics:** AGI discourse; contested definitions; values; feminist STS; epistemic justice
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Shows that AGI definitions encode incompatible social and political assumptions. Highly relevant to treating AGI as a contested/floating signifier rather than a stable technical category.

### Timnit Gebru & Émile P. Torres — TESCREAL / ideological genealogy work

- **Starting point:** First Monday work on the TESCREAL bundle and longtermist/transhumanist ideological genealogy
- **Topics:** transhumanism; singularitarianism; cosmism; Rationalism; Effective Altruism; longtermism; AGI ideology
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Substantive overlap with the ideological formations around AGI, even where the theoretical framework differs from LaclauGPT. Useful as a competing genealogy and critical-AI framing.

## Computational discourse and political text analysis

### Philip Leifeld & Sebastian Haunss — *Political discourse networks and the conflict over software patents in Europe* (2012)

- **Venue:** European Journal of Political Research 51(3), 382–409
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1475-6765.2011.02003.x
- **Topics:** Discourse Network Analysis; discourse coalitions; hegemony; frames; longitudinal networks
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Important methodological bridge from discourse theory to measurable actor–concept networks. Especially relevant to the optional DNA layer and empirical tests of coalition structure and hegemony.

### Ludovic Rheault & Christopher Cochrane — *Word Embeddings for the Analysis of Ideological Placement in Parliamentary Corpora* (2020)

- **Venue:** Political Analysis 28(1), 112–133
- **Replication code:** https://github.com/lrheault/partyembed
- **Topics:** word embeddings; ideology; parliamentary corpora; political scaling; metadata-aware embeddings
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Strong precedent for using embedding geometry to locate political actors and ideological concepts in a common semantic space, with external validation.

### Kenneth Benoit, Scott De Marchi, Conor Laver, Michael Laver & Jinshuai Ma — *Using large language models to analyze political texts through natural language understanding* (2026)

- **Venue:** American Journal of Political Science
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/ajps.70050
- **Topics:** LLMs; political text analysis; natural-language understanding; validation; scalable qualitative interpretation
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Major methodological reference for using LLMs as replicable political-text analysis instruments and benchmarking them against human expertise and conventional text-as-data methods.

### Michal Mochtak — *Chasing the authoritarian spectre: Detecting authoritarian discourse with large language models* (2025)

- **Venue:** European Journal of Political Research 64(3), 1304–1325
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1111/1475-6765.12740
- **Topics:** political discourse; deep learning; weak supervision; latent concepts; authoritarian discourse
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Useful precedent for turning a theoretically meaningful but latent political-discourse concept into scalable computational classification and then validating it.

## Structuralism, embeddings and LLM theory

### Adam C. Kozlowski — *Computational structuralism: Toward a formal theory of meaning in the age of digital intelligence* (2026)

- **Venue:** Theory and Society 55, article 35
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11186-026-09685-z
- **Topics:** structuralism; relational meaning; LLMs; information theory; culture; computational social science
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Extremely close to the LLM Structuralism argument. Frames LLMs and computational methods as formal-relational models of systems of meaning and explicitly connects deep learning with classical structuralism.

### Elad Vromen — *Language Models as Semiotic Machines: Reconceptualizing AI Language Systems through Structuralist and Post-Structuralist Theories of Language* (2024)

- **Venue:** arXiv:2410.13065
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13065
- **Topics:** Saussure; Derrida; structuralism; post-structuralism; word embeddings; LLMs; semiotics
- **Why read for LaclauGPT:** Direct theoretical support for treating embeddings and LLMs as relational/semiotic systems rather than primarily cognitive models. Highly relevant to the Saussure → embeddings → LLM Structuralism bridge.

## Curation rule

When new literature is found, add it here if it is plausibly relevant to one or more of:

- Laclau, Mouffe, Palonen, hegemony, articulation, antagonism, empty/floating signifiers or chains of equivalence;
- computational discourse analysis, Discourse Network Analysis, frame/coalition/network analysis;
- embeddings, semantic geometry, structuralism, post-structuralism or relational models of meaning;
- LLM-assisted qualitative/political/social-science analysis and validation;
- sociotechnical imaginaries, AI/AGI ideology, Critical AI Studies, accelerationism, x-risk/safety, anti-AI or labor/civil-society AI discourse;
- methods that can triangulate or challenge LaclauGPT outputs, including geometric data analysis, network science, temporal semantic change and human-in-the-loop coding.

Do not treat inclusion here as endorsement or as incorporation into the paper. This is a reading queue and related-work map.

## Multimodal social-semiotic pre-analysis

### Hanna Wanselin, Kristina Danielsson & Susanne Wikman — *Analysing Multimodal Texts in Science—a Social Semiotic Perspective* (2022)

- **Venue:** Research in Science Education 52, 891–907; published online 16 October 2021.
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1007/s11165-021-10027-5

## Laclau and Luhmann: Phase 2 cross-theoretical precedents

These three works are **reading candidates** identified via bibliographic/publisher/author records, not local PDF imports. A source-grounded cross-reading is recorded in [SUMMARIES.md](SUMMARIES.md#laclau–luhmann-dialogue-phase-2-literature-synthesis-25-september-2026); the two uploaded introductions by Moeller (2006) and Baraldi, Corsi and Esposito (2021) are already indexed and summarized there.

### Urs Stäheli — *Politik der Entparadoxierung. Zur Artikulation von Hegemonie- und Systemtheorie* (1998)

- **In:** Oliver Marchart (ed.), *Das Undarstellbare der Politik. Zur Hegemonietheorie Ernesto Laclaus*, Turia & Kant, pp. 52–66.
- **Verification:** [University of Hamburg bibliography](https://www.wiso.uni-hamburg.de/en/fachbereich-sowi/professuren/staeheli/team/lehrstuhl-mitarbeiter/staeheli-urs.html).
- **Why read:** Direct dialogue between hegemony and systems theory; explore paradox/deparadoxification without reducing system/environment distinction to Laclaudian antagonism.

### Bob Jessop — *The relevance of Luhmann's systems theory and of Laclau and Mouffe's discourse analysis to the elaboration of Marx's state theory* (2008)

- **In:** German published chapter in Hirsch, Kannankulam and Wissel (eds.), *Der Staat der bürgerlichen Gesellschaft*, Nomos, pp. 157–179.
- **English preprint:** [Author's site](https://bobjessop.wordpress.com/2014/02/09/the-relevance-of-luhmanns-systems-theory-and-of-laclau-and-mouffes-discourse-analysis-to-the-elaboration-of-marxs-state-theory/).
- **Why read:** Explicit critical comparison of differentiated systems, the political and state-theoretical structures; test where the two accounts complement or conflict.

### Niels Åkerstrøm Andersen — *Discursive Analytical Strategies: Understanding Foucault, Koselleck, Laclau, Luhmann* (2003)

- **Publisher:** Policy Press. ISBN 9781861344397. [Publisher record](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.56687/9781447342205/html).
- **Why read:** Distinct analytical strategies for discourse and systems observation; a methodological model for triangulation without declaring ontological identity.

**Phase boundary:** These are Phase 2 exploratory literature candidates. They do not amend the locked Phase 1 paper or its method.
