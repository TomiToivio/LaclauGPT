# Daily research and development reports

This directory stores concise daily research-and-development briefs for the **LaclauGPT: Ideological contestation over AI** project.

## Source of truth

- `paper/PHASE_1_PAPER.md` is the paper source of truth.
- Reports are secondary working notes and recommendations. They do not override the paper, theory documentation, validation protocol, issues, or code.
- There is no implied paper deadline or countdown unless one is explicitly established in the repository.

## Scope

Daily reports may review:

- meaningful changes to the paper, code, issues, tests, and architecture;
- newly surfaced academic literature relevant to Laclau/Mouffe/Palonen, AC/DT, Critical AI Studies, sociotechnical imaginaries, accelerationism, x-risk, critical-AI and anti-AI discourse;
- LLM-assisted qualitative/discourse-analysis methods and validation;
- local/open LLM workflows and relevant tooling such as 4CAT, Zeeschuimer, DATS, data pipelines, interoperability, and human-review workflows;
- high-signal AI governance and ideology developments that may affect sampling, coding, or interpretation.

Each report should separate high-signal items from background noise, explain why findings matter for the paper or code, and end with concrete follow-up recommendations. Reports must not contain private operational collection targets, research data, credentials, or reviewer-identifying information.

## Naming

Use one Markdown file per day:

`YYYY-MM-DD.md`

Newest reports should be listed first below.
