- **Methodology: definition sensitivity as a reportable property of LLM annotation
  (arXiv 2611.13339, Mamaev, Wang, Leckie & Vergani; 9 Nov; accepted at a NeurIPS 2026
  workshop on trustworthy AI evaluation).** Design: have the model annotate the same
  texts twice under two theoretically defensible competing definitions of a concept;
  compare against a paraphrase floor (same requirements, different wording). Across six
  concepts and three LLMs, swapping definitions always changed more annotations than
  paraphrasing - so annotations depend on the prompted definition, and definition
  sensitivity plus the paraphrase floor should be reported with any set of LLM
  annotations. *Credible (workshop-accepted preprint).* AI26 relevance: a cheap, bounded
  instrument, not a redesign. A double-coding pass with one competing but defensible
  definition on a human-reviewed subsample (e.g. the populism/frontier constructs where
  the design requires evidenced Us/Frontier construction) would give the validation
  report a concrete prompt-robustness number and fits the framework's habit of reporting
  coding-rule ambiguity explicitly. Consistent with the locked Phase 1 methodology, which
  already treats LLM output as candidate interpretations pending human adjudication;
