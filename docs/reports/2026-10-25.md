### New external signals

- **arXiv 2610.30900, "Silicon Samples Match the Answers but Not the
  Respondents"** (22 Oct 2026). Introduces the respondent coverage gap (RCG):
  an LLM-generated survey population can reproduce every per-question answer
  distribution while composing respondents whose *combinations* of answers
  exist in no human sample. Why it matters: a validation vocabulary for any
  synthetic-population or simulated-subject method, and a caution for our own
  corpus-level share claims — agreement at the per-question level does not
  imply agreement at the level of the document or the actor. Phase 1 / 4.2
  validation reference; Phase 3 comparison metrics.

  Auditable LLM Output Quality Enforcement"** (21 Oct 2026). Deterministic

  design — partially-grounded records get typed dispositions rather than
  silent rejection; the dead-letter/quarantine distinction put into production
