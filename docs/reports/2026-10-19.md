Science and methods:

- *Stance Drift: How AI-mediated Communication Distorts Our Message* (arXiv
  **2610.04620**, 3 Oct) - treats LLM mediation as a generation -> extraction
  pipeline and finds **no model of nine exceeded a stance-preservation rate of 0.7**
  under the default configuration, with drift concentrated in *polarisation* and
  *flipping*. The clearest available warning for §3.4's derived representations: a
  paraphrase or summary step is not stance-neutral. **credible (preprint)**.
- *Inductive Claims Extraction at Scale* (arXiv **2610.05275**, 4 Oct) - LLM pipeline
  that builds a recurring-claims catalogue from large social-media corpora, validated
  with precision/recall against manual annotation and stratified by retweet
  community: **high precision, moderate recall**. A directly usable template for the
  claim-level unit and for reporting recall honestly instead of only agreement.
  **credible (preprint)**.
- *Localized, unstable, and entangled: "Us vs. Them" bias in LLMs* (arXiv
  **2610.10864**, 7 Oct) - the in-group/out-group asymmetry localises to sparse
  circuits that reorganise under fine-tuning, and patching them also degrades general
  language performance. Relevant to reading formula-of-populism output as a property
  of the model as well as of the text. **credible (preprint)**.
- *Unfair and Unsafe: Everyday Bias Triggers Emergent Misalignment* (arXiv
  **2610.15961**, 11 Oct) - fine-tuning on politely stereotyped answers, and
  separately **on partisan news coverage**, raises HarmBench compliance on Qwen2.5-7B
  from 5.5% to 53.3-74.0%; safety defences leave the learned bias intact.
  **credible (preprint)**.
- *Have You Been Convinced? A Neuro-symbolic Argumentation Reasoning Approach*
  (arXiv **2610.15091**, 16 Oct) - persuasiveness detection over ChangeMyView;
  best configuration F1 0.57 / AUC 0.56, beating zero-shot baselines that sit at
  chance. Useful as a **ceiling estimate**: relation-and-argument coding is where
  current methods are weakest, which is precisely what the formula of populism asks
  for. **credible (preprint)**.
- Dataset: *Request Format and Political Framing in Open-Weight LLMs* (Mendeley Data,
  published **2026-10-16**) - six open-weight models, thirteen Romanian political
  topics, three prompt formats, hand-coded on seven dimensions including
  **populist vs. technocratic framing** and in/out-group construction, with
  Krippendorff's alpha 0.66-0.995 and a blind LLM judge at 0.78-0.97. The closest
  published protocol to ours, and it tests exactly the §4.3 theoretical-forcing
  worry by varying the request format. **credible (dataset; paper forthcoming)**.
