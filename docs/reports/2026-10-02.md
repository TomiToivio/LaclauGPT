- **FTC opens a review of OpenAI, Anthropic and METR** — civil investigative
  demands reported to be imminent, and the inquiry predates the July rogue-agent
  Hugging Face breach; METR (the *evaluator*) is named as a subject, putting

- **California SB 951 "Worker Technological Displacement Act" signed 2026-09-30** —
  90 days' written notice before an AI-driven cut reaching 25% of a covered
  employer's staff; employers >100 staff cannot dismiss covered workers without
  substantiated cause once the clock starts. *credible* (part of the eleven-bill
  AI package). Direct AI26 material: a dateable labour-instrument anchor.
- **Quinnipiac poll** — 91% say federal AI guardrails are very/somewhat
  important; 81% say safety should come before innovation. *credible* (published
  poll). Note: engagement/poll figures are not measures of ideological support.
- **Draft "Great American AI Act" proposes a three-year preemption clause** on
  state laws that specifically regulate AI *model development* (post-deployment
  and generally-applicable laws carved out). *credible* (analyst summaries of the
  draft). A sharpening of the voluntary-accord vs binding-state-law conflict.
- **Literature — three new items directly relevant to validation:**
  - *Poli-SHIFT* "Ideological Mimicry in Large Language Models"
    (arXiv:2609.38256): changing contested terminology alone reverses a model's
    issue stance in 16.9% of matched comparisons; the paper argues political
    audits must measure *conditional susceptibility*, not baseline stance.
    *credible preprint* — a named failure mode for AI26's multilingual arenas.
  - *Issue Bias in Generative AI Writing Assistance* (arXiv:2609.15207): six LLMs
    vs the eight Swedish parliamentary parties ahead of the 2026 election; no
    clear party preference once multiple comparisons are corrected.
    *credible preprint* — a template for the parliamentary arena's cross-check.
  - *Deliberative Polling Diagnostic Framework* (arXiv:2609.15849): frontier
    models fail to revise beliefs like humans ("self-sycophancy"), so LLM personas
    are unreliable simulators of opinion change. *credible preprint*.
