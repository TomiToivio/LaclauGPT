only in `capture_server.py:378-401`, i.e. only on the browser path this node never runs.

All twelve non-fallback rows hold exactly one record ever, zero in 14 days, while the pages answer
200 with 22-301 anchors from Laskin (table with per-source anchor counts in #321). Ruled out: bot

### Literature watch (22:00 run)

Three preprints new to the register (all **credible, preprint**; none already logged):

- **arXiv 2610.16498 - "From Caricature to Collective Artifact: How Persona Construction Drives
  Polarization in Generative Social Simulations"** (Michailidis, Tornberg & Santos, 12 Oct,
  cs.SI). On a Twitter-like generative social simulation populated with ANES-derived personas,
  ablating three layers of partisan identity shows removing a few explicitly partisan statements
  moves the network from near-complete segregation to cross-party ties approaching real Twitter
  - and that surveys convert positions into behavioural instructions for personas regardless of
  salience. Why it matters: this is the controlled critique of "LLM personas simulate a
  population", the exact assumption behind any synthetic-persona move in AI26; it converts the
  10-07 'Algorithmic Caricature' logging into a concrete ablation design, and its salience
  argument is directly relevant to how the paper's demands/aggregations are modelled (salience
  is part of articulation, not noise). Phase 2.
- **arXiv 2610.20360 - "Do AI Agents Gossip? Evaluative Talk in Human and AI-Agent Online
  Communities"** (Hua, Lin & Hsieh, 14 Oct, cs.SI/cs.CL). Compares evaluator talk about absent
  third parties on Moltbook (AI agents) against PTT (Taiwan) with one operational definition:
  gossip occurs on Moltbook at 2.6-6x lower prevalence, targeting locally prominent figures on
  both platforms. Why it matters: rare direct evidence on whether AI-agent communities reproduce
  human discourse practices - machine-communication material for Phase 2's content/network
  bridge rather than a methods import. Phase 2.
- **arXiv 2610.26412 - "Where Does Rhetorical Distortion Live? A Multi-Platform, Multi-Language
  Measurement of Influencer Content"** (Yu, Chen & Wang, 19 Oct, cs.CL). Operationalises
  disproportionate framing (not falsity) on five dimensions - Significance Inflation, Anxiety
  Manufacturing, Novelty Claims, Loaded Language, Temporal Distortion - via a three-tier
