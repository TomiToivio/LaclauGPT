   a factor of two.

### Scientific publications

Three items meeting the bar, all on the validation method side, none
previously carried:

- **arXiv 2611.00256, "Validating LLM-Generated Synthetic Survey Samples: A
  Use-Dependent Multi-Dimensional Framework for Social-Science Applications"**
  (30 Oct, econ.GN). Validates synthetic Eurobarometer response samples on AI
  attitudes against three distinct notions - predictive, distributional and
  substantive - and finds respondent-level weakness coexisting with
  preserved marginal heterogeneity, demographic ordering and cross-country
  structure; evaluation must be indexed to the intended use. *Established
  (preprint).* This is a citation-ready frame for the paper's human-in-the-loop
  validation protocol: LLM pre-analysis outputs are preliminary claims whose
  validity is dimensional, not scalar, and Phase 3's validation manifest
  should name which dimension each check bears on. **Phase 2/3.**
- **arXiv 2611.01354, "Wrong Together: Shared Bias in User Simulation"**
  (3 Nov, cs.CL). Across 6 simulators and GlobalOpinionQA in 12 countries,
  inter-simulator disagreement predicts invalid between-group estimates
  (AUC 0.87) but cannot see errors the simulators share; shared-bias
  subtraction is tested as a partial correction. *Established (preprint).*
  Directly relevant twice: to any multi-model robustness check §4 might use
  ensemble agreement as a cheap validity signal with a named blind spot
  (shared-training-corpus bias), and to Phase 2's agent-simulation plans,
  where ensemble disagreement monitoring is now an evidenced, low-cost
  guardrail. **Phase 2.**
- **arXiv 2611.04968, "Who is the Pronoun Pro? Comparing LLMs and Humans at
  Number-Disambiguation of 'they'"** (3 Nov, cs.CL). 73 humans and >40 LLM
  configurations annotate 174 authentic uses; the best LLM (94.5%) beats
  human majority vote (86.2%) while the qualitative analysis shows human
  reasoning on low-agreement cases improves the annotation typology itself.
  *Established (preprint, benchmark release with gold labels and full
  annotations).* The finding is not "LLMs beat annotators" but the
  complementarity claim: quantitative superiority coexists with the need for
  human critical oversight to keep the codebook honest - the paper's
  traceability principle rendered as a working demonstration.
  **Phase 1 (as validation-discussion material only; no method change).**
