## AI26 external signals (2026-11-19)

**Scientific (none of these appear in yesterday's brief; 2611.09457 was already covered there
and is not repeated):**

- **Codebook auditing as a method.** *When F1 Is Not Enough: Auditing Codebooks for LLM-Assisted
  Annotation* (arXiv 2611.08846, 6 Nov 2026): class-specific metrics, confusion matrices and
  document-level review attribute annotation errors to *codebook* defects, and targeted
  revisions cut the diagnosed errors on held-out data; some revisions transfer across models,
  others are annotator-specific. Directly usable as the Phase 1 §4.2 codebook-revision
  protocol. *Established (preprint).*
- **Definition sensitivity must be reported.** *Measuring Definition Sensitivity in LLM
  Annotation of Social Science Concepts* (arXiv 2611.13339, 9 Nov 2026): swapping between
  theoretically defensible definitions changes more annotations than paraphrasing the same
  definition, by 1.8-7.0x the paraphrase floor, across six concepts and three models —
  including Gemma 4 31B. Implication for LaclauGPT: a formation codebook should report
  definition sensitivity alongside agreement, because "which definition was in the prompt" is
  part of the measurement instrument. *Credible (preprint, directly on our construct).*
- **Order without magnitude.** *Knowing Which, Not How Much: Language Models Order Party
  Positions They Cannot Place* (arXiv 2611.21878, 16 Nov 2026): models rank parties better
  than they place a single party on a scale, and supplying the coded values recovers
  placement. Implication: parliamentary-arena claims should stay ordinal unless anchored.
  *Credible (preprint).*
- **Ambiguity as a diagnostic, not noise.** *Evaluating Cultural Reasoning and Robustness in
  LLMs for Spanish Political Discourse* (arXiv 2611.21535, 16 Nov 2026): three independent
  human annotation views plus sentiment-flipped counterfactuals expose failure modes that
  original-corpus accuracy hides. Supports the paper's existing stance on disagreement and
  extends it to a non-English arena. *Credible (preprint).*
