### The X stratum has never reached the analysis queue - and it is 30% of the ready corpus

Every platform except X sits at 86-97% of its ready records analysed. X is at **0 of 1,172**.
This is not the #368 capture outage (that one is about production) and not a text problem:
the X rows carry text, and 1,172 of them are marked ready.

The mechanism is a `run_id` mismatch between the handoff envelope and the consumer query:

- `storage/remote.py:_run_id()` stamps `handoff.run_id` from `record.provenance[-1].run_id`.
  Browser captures set that to the **capture run** (`20261022T143705Z`, `20260916T193510Z`);
  all 1,129 newest ready X rows carry `20261022T143705Z`.
- `distributed_worker.py:ready_handoff_query()` filters on
  `handoff.run_id == manifest.run_id` (`ai26-distributed-001`) before seeding a task, and
  `ready_handoffs()` is called with exactly that run id (line 770).
- Consequence: no X record can ever be seeded. The only X-hosted URLs that have ever been
  analysed (9) are `web` records, i.e. external links found inside RSS articles and fetched
  as web sources - X discourse enters the analysis sideways or not at all.

For the paper this is the sharpest current gap: the elites arena's social-media layer, the
whole of the platform-specific part of `sampling_focus`, and 30.7% of the analysis-ready
corpus are absent from every result set. Filed as **#407** (Data-Analysis); not fixed here,
because it changes the Analysis handoff contract and needs the run-id semantics decided.
It also survives #368: a resumed capture producer would stamp capture-run ids too, so every
new X record would be equally unclaimable.
