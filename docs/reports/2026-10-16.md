## AI26 external signals (2026-10-16)

One line each; only material that is new against the 2026-10-15 sections.

Science and methods:

- *ChronoCulture: Evaluating Cultural Awareness Across Time in Large Language Models*
  (arXiv **2610.20472**, 14 Oct) - 10,360 temporal survey pairs, 72 countries, full
  response distributions as ground truth: LLMs often recover a country's attitudes
  at one point in time but **do not track how those attitudes change**, and naming
  the intended year in the prompt barely helps. A direct caution for reading any
  period-specific discourse position off a model. **credible (preprint)**.
- *When Extremists Enter LLM Societies: Investigating Extreme Behavior in
  Multi-Agent Systems* (arXiv **2610.19863**, 14 Oct) - the EXAM framework for
  configuring tail/extremist agents in LLM multi-agent systems (population share,
  spatial distribution, interaction protocol), arguing that persistent tail actors
  shape collective dynamics and cannot be reduced to fixed scalar states. Relevant
  to the simulation strand and to §2.3's competing-formation picture.
  **credible (preprint)**.
- *From Caricature to Collective Artifact: How Persona Construction Drives
  Polarization in Generative Social Simulations* (arXiv **2610.16498**, 12 Oct) -
  personas built from ANES responses: removing a few explicitly partisan statements
  flips the simulated network from near-complete segregation to substantial
  cross-party ties. The sharpest available demonstration of "the categories the
  researcher supplies drive the output", i.e. the §4.3 theoretical-forcing risk.
  **credible (preprint)**.
