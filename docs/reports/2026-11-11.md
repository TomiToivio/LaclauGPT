### The evaluation frame is not converging, and the mechanism is in the seed query

Ready frame 4,708 documents; 3,123 distinct analysed URLs; **1,585 ready but never analysed**
= x 1,169 (closed stratum) + **bluesky 307** + arxiv 52 + rss 35 + mastodon 19 + web 3.
Non-X never-analysed: **416**, of which **295 were published in October** and 36 in
September. Analysed share by platform: rss 98.1 %, web 98.8 %, arxiv 80.4 %, mastodon
75.0 %, **bluesky 71.3 %** (72.0 % yesterday), x 0.3 %.

Mechanism, verified from the running code and the live store rather than inferred:
`MongoCollectionHandoff.ready_handoffs()` sorts
`(source_priority ASC, published_at DESC, source_url ASC)` and the seeder publishes at most
10 tasks per cycle. In that order the ready frame is P1 = positions 1-2,126, P2 = 2,127-3,536,
P3 (X) = 3,537-4,708, and the first never-analysed document sits at **bluesky position
2,127**, mastodon 2,293, arxiv 2,563. Today's cycles show `examined` 1,850 -> 2,130 -> 2,130
with `published` 10 and `already_analyzed` 2,076: the walk reaches the P1/P2 boundary and the
budget is spent there. Because daily P1 intake (40-57 documents) is of the same order as
daily capacity (117 completions, of which 16 duplicate), the P2 tail is never reached.

For §4.2 that matters more than the raw count: the stratified evaluation sample can only be
drawn from analysed documents, and Bluesky is now one of only two reachable social platforms
(the X stratum is closed). A platform whose analysed share *falls* while its ready count
rises cannot supply a stable stratum. Recorded as **Data-Analysis #429** with reproduction,
the ordering evidence and the two acceptable resolutions (drain the tail, or declare the
priority-ordered frame as a limitation in §4.2).
