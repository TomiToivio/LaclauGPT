### Defect found and fixed this run: the preflight hid the cause

The analysis preflight reported only `type(exc).__name__` for object-store failures. Every S3
refusal is a `botocore` `ClientError`, so the provider code was discarded: for 59 cycles the
log said `"error": "ClientError"` and `["object store unreachable", "object store is not
writeable"]` for a store that was up and full. The collector was naming `QuotaExceeded` per
object the whole time; the gate the reports quote was not.

- Issue: **Data-Analysis #408**
- Fix: **PR #409** - `_s3_failure_detail()` carries `error_code` and `http_status` from the
  botocore response (no message, URL, key or credential), quota gets its own problem line,
  other provider codes are appended, and `object store unreachable` now means a real
  transport failure. Fail-closed behaviour unchanged.
- Verified live, not only in tests: the same check against the running configuration now
  returns `error_code: QuotaExceeded, http_status: 403`.

### Scientific research

Freshness check first: **2609.26926** (cross-model disagreement routing) was
already registered 2026-09-27 and **2609.23039** (speech-regime audit)
2026-10-06 - they resurfaced in tonight's sweep and are not re-registered.
Three genuinely new items, all verified against the abstract source:

- **arXiv 2610.18588, "Decomposing and Predicting Annotation Disagreement in
  Harmful Content Across Languages"** (13 Oct 2026). Predicts *which items
  will be contested before annotation*: an LLM answers interpretable binary
  questions about the text (derogation, missing context, decision
  boundaries, language-sensitive cues), a classifier turns those answers
  into a disagreement prediction. Structured-question features beat direct
  prompting by 10.5 ROC-AUC points and text-based baselines by 5.0; a
  compact 8-question subset performs within 1.5 points of the full 20;
  leave-one-dataset-out keeps 90-95% of in-domain AUC across English,
  German, Turkish. Why it matters: it complements the already-registered
  post-annotation routing idea (2609.26926 routes on *observed* cross-model
  disagreement) with a *pre-annotation* predictor - cheap, interpretable,
  transferable across datasets. For AI26 this is a concrete design for
  spending scarce human-review effort on the claims most likely to be
  contested, which is the operational core of the human-in-the-loop
  contract. 2610-13: not previously registered. *Phase 2 (methods).*
- **arXiv 2603.21404, "Multi-Perspective LLM Annotations for Valid Analyses
  in Subjective Tasks"** (Perspective-Driven Inference; v2 surfacing in
  search now, original March 2026). Treats the *distribution* of
  annotations across demographic groups - not a single ground truth - as
  the quantity of interest, and adaptively concentrates the human
  annotation budget on the groups where LLM proxies are least accurate
  (evaluated on politeness/offensiveness). Why it matters: the third leg of
  the routing family - after routing by disagreement (26926) and predicting
  disagreement (18588), this one asks *whose* labels LLMs proxy worst and
  allocates human effort there. Directly maps onto AI26's strata: coding
  reliability will not be uniform across elites/grassroots/parliamentary
  records or across actors within them, and review budgets should follow
  measured proxy weakness. *Phase 2 (methods).*
- **arXiv 2610.37970, "Statistical Detectability of Coordination"**
  (Iannucci, Matakos, **Mikko Kivelä**, 27 Oct 2026). Formulates social-media
  coordination detection as pairwise hypothesis testing against explicit
  null models, connects the scores to planted-submatrix detection, and
  derives statistical and computational limits as functions of group size,
  population and signal strength; validated signal estimates across 7
  coordination modalities on 26 labeled information-operation datasets.
  Key finding for us: **the choice of null model changes the detectability
  regime of most modalities** - heuristic agreement with ground truth is
  regime-dependent, so "coordinated behaviour" claims are only as good as
  the stated null. Why it matters: AI26's network-level analysis (Castells
  side) will be tempted by coordination/amplification reads of the X and
  Bluesky strata. This paper is the formal guardrail: co-activation is not
  coordination without a null model, and in small or sparse strata detection
  may be statistically impossible at all. Notably a Finnish co-author. *Phase 2
  (validation design), Phase 3 (any coalition/amplification claim).*

One low-priority flag: **arXiv 2610.40655, "Elucidating the Design Space of

attribution; redundancy and lexical distractors consistently degrade
