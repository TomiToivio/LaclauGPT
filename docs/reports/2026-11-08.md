### New today: analysis throughput has halved, and the cause is truncation cascades

This is the one genuinely new technical finding of the day, and it is a change in behaviour
rather than a fault. Completions per cycle fell from a stable 9-10 on 2026-11-07 to 4-6 in the
last three cycles, while retries rose from 0-1 to 3-5 and two documents were dead-lettered in
30 hours:

| cycle end | completed | retry | dead-letter | claims |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11-07 17:24 | 10 | 0 | 0 | 11 |
| 11-07 19:15 | 10 | 0 | 0 | 10 |
| 11-07 21:10 | 9 | 1 | 0 | 10 |
| 11-07 23:24 | 7 | 3 | 0 | 10 |
| 11-08 01:14 | 8 | 1 | 1 | 13 |
| 11-08 03:09 | 9 | 1 | 0 | 11 |
| 11-08 04:54 | 6 | 4 | 0 | 10 |
| 11-08 06:00 | 4 | 5 | 1 | 13 |

The mechanism is visible in the prompt budget line: as the records being analysed grew longer,
the prompt saturates the 48,000-char budget (39,690 used) with `current_source` capped at
20,000 chars and `previous_analysis` at 12,000, and `situational`/`memory`/`rag` omitted
entirely. The answer then hits `finish_reason=length` at `num_predict=4096` and again at 8192,
so a single document costs two to four model calls and the 10-task cycle budget buys far fewer
completed analyses. The 05:05 cycle alone logged 9 truncations (5 at 4096, 4 at 8192) and 7
parse failures.

Today's truncation/parse traffic per cycle: 00:05 = 2/6, 02:05 = 0/2, 04:05 = 4/0, 05:05 = 9/7.
Total for the day so far: 15 truncations, 15 parse failures, against whole-log totals of 1,346
at `num_predict=4096`, 1,253 at 8192 and 101 at 16384. These lines are still per-record retry
traffic and not a stopped pipeline - but at this rate they now consume about a third of the
cycle, which is why the completions fell.

Opened **Data-Analysis #425** with the reproduction, the per-cycle table and the three options
(raise the output ceiling, lower the source cap so the answer fits, or accept ~50 documents a
day). The choice is a research-quality trade-off - it decides how much source text the model
sees - so it is left for a human rather than changed silently.
