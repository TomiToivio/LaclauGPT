### Phase-specific implications
**Phase 1 (locked research plan and implementation).** Preserve Laclau/Mouffe, Palonen, sociotechnical imaginaries, Critical AI Studies and human-reviewed LLM-assisted analysis. Prioritize observed-versus-declared source/arena/language coverage, freshness, deduplication and traceable exclusions; #88 and #400 remain critical. No Phase 2 retrofit.

**Phase 2 (advanced theory and implementation).** Keep **Laclau** (content/semantic articulation), **Leifeld DNA** (actor–concept–statement positions) and **Castells** (observed communication network) separate, connected by source IDs and reversible transformations. Luhmann, Deleuze and Haraway inform network/assemblage interpretation; Faggin and Hoffman remain non-empirical metaphysical background. Test whether long-input truncation changes claims, stance and network edges before adjusting model budgets. Never treat simulated interactions as observed ties.

**Phase 3 (preliminary results).** Create a reproducibility manifest: corpus snapshot, observed strata, language/arena missingness, collection gaps, model/prompt/code versions, truncation/retry/dead-letter rates, human adjudication and uncertainty. A passing pipeline alone does not warrant comparative findings.

**Phase 4 (final results).** Preserve a frozen chain from source evidence through validated discourse annotations, DNA/SNA transformations, reviewed figures and final conclusions. No deadline inferred.
