### Phase-by-phase assessment

- **Phase 1 — locked research plan and technical implementation:** Maintain Laclau/Mouffe discourse theory, Palonen's Formula of Populism, sociotechnical imaginaries, traceable evidence and human review as articulated in the accessible Phase 1 manuscript. Priority: validate text sufficiency, language abstentions, corpus coverage, version parity and persistence. No theoretical or methodological redesign.
- **Phase 2 — advanced communication-network theory/method:** Keep **Laclau = content of communication**, **Leifeld DNA = actor–statement–stance bridge**, **Castells = networks of communication**. Use Luhmann, Deleuze and Haraway/cyborg/assemblage thinking as complementary lenses; Faggin/Hoffman only as speculative metaphysical background. Next: define and test canonical actor IDs, claim IDs, stance, provenance, typed edges, temporal semantics and projection rules across Analysis → Visualization. Do not backport Phase 2 premises into Phase 1.
- **Phase 3 — intermediate results:** Prepare a versioned, non-sensitive reproducibility manifest: effective corpus and missingness, text sufficiency, language uncertainty, codebook/model/prompt revisions, retries and write failures, human adjudication, and sensitivity to sampling decisions. The present green CI does not by itself validate empirical interpretation.
- **Phase 4 — final results:** Hold substantive conclusions and final network figures until complete source-to-code-to-edge-to-figure traceability and cross-module reproducibility are demonstrated.
