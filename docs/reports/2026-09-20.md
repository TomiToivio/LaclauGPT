# LaclauGPT / AI26 daily R&D brief — 2026-09-20

## High signal

### Phase 1 — the source of truth remains locked; implementation is catching up

The repository currently names the Phase 1 source-of-truth file `paper/PHASE_1_PAPER.md`. Its method remains Laclau/Mouffe + Palonen, sociotechnical imaginaries, Critical AI Studies, and evidence-linked LLM-assisted pre-analysis with human interpretation and validation. Phase 2 concepts must not rewrite this method.

The most important newly surfaced Phase 1 design item is issue #61, **Social-semiotic multimodal/text pre-analysis before discourse analysis**. It proposes a bounded descriptive first pass based primarily on Kress/van Leeuwen-style multimodal social semiotics, with Hallidayan and light structuralist support, before LLM Structuralism and Laclau analysis. This is compatible with the locked Phase 1 method only if it remains preprocessing: it may describe semiotic resources, participants/processes/circumstances, composition, intermodal relations, observable contrasts/repetitions and uncertainty, but must not infer nodal/empty/floating signifiers, equivalential chains, antagonisms, ideology, populism or political subjects.

**Why it matters:** this gives multimodal preprocessing a defensible social-science method while preserving the boundary between description and political interpretation. It also creates a clean provenance boundary between direct source evidence, OCR/ASR/vision-derived description, and downstream Laclaudian interpretation.

**Follow-up:** implement one modality-agnostic pre-analysis contract with explicit evidence pointers, modality provenance, uncertainty and prompt/schema version. Add negative acceptance tests proving that political/discourse categories cannot leak into this stage.

### Phase 1 — knowledge graph remains infrastructure, not theory

The RDF/knowledge-graph spine added across Collection, Analysis and Visualization remains the strongest architectural development from the previous cycle. Collection's RDF/JSON-LD/SHACL boundary, Analysis DNA/SNA projections, and Visualization's backend-neutral graph contract should be connected through a single synthetic end-to-end fixture.

**Guardrail:** graph topology, centrality, co-occurrence, semantic drift or RDF predicates are measurements/representations, not automatic evidence of Laclaudian nodal points, floating/empty signifiers, equivalence, antagonism or hegemony.

### Phase 1 — no new code movement after the last report; use the pause for integration

No repository commit newer than the late-19-September main-repository source-summary update was observed in the four public repositories at this review. The latest additional main-repo change adds summaries of Alexander Galloway's *The Interface Effect* and *Protocol*. This is useful conceptual material, especially for Phase 2's mediated communication/network layer, but it should not expand Phase 1's locked method.

The engineering priority therefore remains integration rather than feature growth: Collection → canonical record → social-semiotic preprocessing where applicable → Phase 1 analysis → evidence/provenance → visualization/review.

### Phase 2 — Galloway is a useful bridge, not a replacement for Castells/Luhmann/DNA

The newly added Galloway summaries are well placed for Phase 2. *Protocol* helps conceptualize distributed technical control, while *The Interface Effect* foregrounds interfaces as active mediators rather than transparent windows. They can sharpen the analysis of AI-era communication infrastructures and platform mediation.

Keep the core Phase 2 division stable:

- Content of Communication: Laclau
- Discourse-network bridge: Leifeld DNA
- Network of Communication: Castells
- Luhmann/Deleuze/Haraway/Galloway: supporting perspectives on systems, mediation, assemblage and technical organization

Faggin/Hoffman remain metaphysical background rather than empirical premises.

**Follow-up:** when Phase 2 implementation resumes, distinguish observed communication ties, platform/protocol mediation, DNA actor–concept stance relations and Laclaudian semantic/articulatory relations as separate typed layers with reversible provenance.

### Phase 3 — preliminary results need temporal snapshots

Phase 3's immediate dependency is not additional theory but a frozen, inspectable analytical snapshot. Preserve corpus version, source coverage, software commits, schema/prompt/model/runtime versions, codebook/context fingerprints, human-review status, exclusions and failures.

### Phase 4 — design the final freeze before needing it

Phase 4 should inherit the validated instrument rather than trigger a late architectural rewrite. Define archival exports and figure/table provenance early so final results can be reconstructed from immutable source and run manifests.

Reuters reports that U.S.–China economic talks on 20 September include AI regulation and guardrails, including questions around open- and closed-weight models, alongside trade and critical minerals. ### Validation — evidence increasingly supports decomposition + provenance + human adjudication

Forster et al. (2026) present a four-stage LLM qualitative-analysis pipeline of segmentation, coding, concept development and quote extraction with a complete audit trail and expert validation. Pellegrini & Spinuzzi (2026) find LLM coding useful but vulnerable to keyword-driven misclassification and granularity problems. Liu et al. (2026), *Agreement Is Not Quality*, show why agreement with human coders is not sufficient as the sole quality criterion. Misra et al. (2026) add a useful local/open-model caution: Gemma2 and Llama 3.1 produced substantial duplication and limited contextual alignment in their qualitative-analysis setting.

**Phase 1 implication:** validation should combine mechanical provenance/evidence checks, agreement where appropriate, blinded/adjudicated qualitative assessment, characteristic-error analysis, and explicit abstention/uncertainty. Local model choice should be empirically validated per task rather than assumed equivalent.

## Critical AI / cyborg position

The programme remains productively located inside Critical AI Studies while retaining a Haraway/cyborg/assemblage openness to technological transformation. Critical analysis supplies the questions of power, labour, ownership, inequality, infrastructure, governance and exclusion; the cyborg/assemblage orientation prevents critique from assuming in advance that technological transformation is necessarily emancipatory or necessarily domination.

PCM remains nearby conceptual inspiration at programme level. Its specific metaphysics and political theory do not enter locked Phase 1 unless the Phase 1 paper itself introduces them.

## Background noise / avoid

Do not expand Phase 1 theory because Galloway or Phase 2 network theory is interesting. Do not let social semiotics become a covert political-framing classifier. Do not turn RDF into an ontology that predetermines empirical findings. Do not treat `safety`, `openness`, `control`, `sovereignty` or `acceleration` as ideologically owned by one camp. Do not equate LLM-human agreement with validity.

## Bottom line

Today's strongest methodological development is the move toward a **social-semiotic descriptive layer before Laclaudian analysis**. Done carefully, it solves a real weakness: multimodal evidence can be described systematically without smuggling political interpretation into preprocessing. The strongest empirical signal is equally relational: AI `safety` now travels through diplomacy, antitrust, sovereignty, competition and acceleration arguments. Both developments point in the same direction: preserve layers, preserve provenance, and analyse relations rather than forcing the material into premature categories.
