### Research watch: the annotation-validation cluster got its third leg

Three preprints today, completing a series the reports have been tracking
since 6 November (2611.08846, 2611.09457). All are Phase 1-relevant method
watch, none change the locked framework:

- **"From DQI to C-DQI" (Shizgal, Keinan et al., arXiv 2610.27937, surfaced
  today via search)** - a complete protocol for LLM-based codebook generation
  and evaluation for computational discourse quality, comparing top-down
  (theory-based), bottom-up and hybrid codebook induction on 100 Knesset
  transcripts, with an inter-model-agreement (IMA) metric that uses differing
  model biases as a reliability signal. Was recorded in the 13 November
  annex; today's search confirms it as the parliamentary-discourse anchor of
  the codebook-audit cluster (Phase 1 validation comparator, Phase 2
  candidate when schema induction meets formation coding).
- **"High Scores, Hidden Failures: Evaluating Codebook-Based LLM Content
  Analysis" (arXiv 2610.35179, not previously recorded)** - large-scale
  failure-mode analysis across four datasets and five models: high aggregate
  metrics coexist with severe label-level disparities, run-to-run instability,
  domain sensitivity and label-interpretation drift, all invisible under
  accuracy/micro-F1. It proposes exactly the minimal validation screen
  (macro-F1 + worst-label-F1, label-swap meaning-adherence probes, >=3 inference
  repeats, formatting perturbations) that fits LaclauGPT's formation coding
  and its human-in-the-loop floor. Phase 1 validation design: the strongest
  practical checklist in the cluster; its label-swap probe operationalises
  the same threat 2611.09457 demonstrated. Not previously in the reports.
- **"Who Writes the Codebook?" (2611.19872, recorded 20 November)** - already
  in the register; nothing new to add beyond tonight's confirmation that the
  between-model agreement figure (.69) lands in the same range the
  stability screen above treats as the sensitivity floor.

The chain is now: *what governs the output* (09457), *what audits the
codebook* (08846), *what the aggregate metrics hide* (35179), *who wrote the
categories* (19872), *what parliamentary schema induction looks like*
(27937). For the Phase 1 validation section this is effectively a
pre-assembled related-work paragraph: the project's own claim - that LLM
labels are preliminary evidence pending human adjudication - is now the
consensus position of a five-paper research front, which strengthens the
paper's framing without requiring any framework change.

Also noted, background: **"Never Break the Chain: Revisiting Chains of
