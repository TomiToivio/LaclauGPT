### Research watch: instrument uncertainty

- **Reiter, Kern, Miasnikov, Nikolenko, Chew, Eckman and Kreuter: "Reliable but
  Design-Sensitive: Instrument Uncertainty in LLM Annotation" (UncertaiNLP 2026
  workshop proceedings, pp. 312-327; arXiv 2609.35824, 22 Sep, surfaced today).**
  Seven LLMs, 12 task designs, 3 independent runs, 3,000 tweets on offensive language
  and hate speech: reliability *within* one task design is high (median Fleiss'
  kappa = 0.91) while agreement *across* defensible designs falls to median Cohen's
  kappa = 0.76; task design and model choice inflate the variance of estimated
  prevalence 76.7x / 110.6x over nominal sampling variance, with design effects
  (560-572 bp) larger than the variation across five human instrument versions
  (270-331 bp); confidence scores track repetition rather than correctness. Estimated
