  ~80%. Still unreported at the time of writing: Lucas County (Oregon, OH),
  Monroe and Sprigg Townships (Adams County), Monroeville. *Established (unofficial

  final certified counts not yet available - treat the "at least 14" as a floor,
  not a total.)* This is the first at-scale popular vote on the object, and it is
  the largest single grassroots-arena event in the corpus' observation period.

  campaign that argued for ending data-centre tax breaks and making operators pay

  *Established (same reporting).* Analytically this is the cleanest available case

  require frontier labs to publish the share of R&D compute dedicated to safety,
  with Independent Verification Organisations checking the figures and civil
  enforcement for violations. *Credible (a16z AI Policy Brief, 2026-11-02).* A new
  articulation of safety as a *disclosure ratio* rather than a standard - relevant

  cs.CY).** Preregistered, five judge models across three providers re-judged
  4,000 Chatbot Arena battles: median Cohen's kappa against the human vote 0.22,

  preprint, not peer-reviewed).* This bites §4.2 directly: the paper's robustness

  model", and this study shows a cross-provider judge swap is not a reliability
  guarantee. It should be cited in the validation section, and the abstention /
  agreement reporting should name which judge produced which code.

  risk at all, and five risk categories account for over half of all
