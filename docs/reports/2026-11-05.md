### Scientific publications (5 November sweep)

- **arXiv 2611.05702, "TACT: A Pragmatic Approach to Qualitative Analysis with
  an AI Council"** (Choudhuri, Bird, Badea, Sarma; OSU/Microsoft Research;
  cs.SE, 3 Nov). A bounded-delegation AI council for thematic analysis:
  three model families independently discover candidate codes with verbatim
  evidence (deterministically checked), a reconciler merges the codebook,
  researchers code ~10% and approve it, three coder models then code with
  rationales, and majority vote plus an uncertainty-surfacing decision model
  close the loop. F1 0.75-0.77 on an independently labeled benchmark, κ
  0.79-0.85 on a held-out sample, $13.77-33.59 per 100 threads. *Established
  (preprint).* TACT is one of the closest published architectures to
  LaclauGPT's working pipeline to date: verbatim-evidence grounding,
  deterministic verification, human-approved codebook before coding,
  per-assignment rationales, majority/decision-model consensus are all
  LaclauGPT design commitments now independently arriving in the literature.
  Its measured agreement levels are a Phase 3 calibration reference for §4.2's
  agreement targets, and its staged adversaries/decision-model idea is a
  Phase 2 comparison point for disagreement handling - reading, not adoption.
