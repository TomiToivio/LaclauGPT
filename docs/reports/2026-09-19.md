### Phase 1 — protect the locked method while integrating the pipeline

The most consequential new implementation work is the appearance of a cross-repository RDF/knowledge-graph path. Data Analysis implemented RDF/knowledge-graph output with DNA and SNA projections; Data Collection added a deliberately bounded collection-side RDF/JSON-LD/SHACL projection; Data Visualization added a backend-neutral graph contract and bounded researcher exploration operations. This is useful infrastructure, but it creates a new methodological guardrail: **RDF/graph representation must remain a representation of Phase 1 evidence and analysis, not a substitute theory of discourse.** Graph topology, centrality, co-occurrence or RDF predicates must not be allowed to redefine nodal points, floating/empty signifiers, equivalence/difference, antagonism, demands, frontiers or imaginaries.

Collection's boundary is especially healthy: analytical constructs remain downstream, the KG path is disabled by default, and SHACL/schema work focuses on metadata/provenance. Preserve that boundary.

**Follow-up:** add one cross-repository synthetic fixture that proves the same source/evidence identity and provenance survive Collection → Analysis → RDF/JSON-LD → Visualization, including human-review state and reversible evidence links. Treat this as a Phase 1 representation/provenance test, not a new method.

**Content of Communication → Laclau**
**DNA bridge → Leifeld**

## Research watch

### High signal: AI safety is becoming an institutional field, not only a discourse camp

On 18 September, Anthropic and Accenture announced a five-year embedded-evaluation partnership, with each expecting to invest at least $1 billion. Reuters reports the arrangement as an effort to independently evaluate frontier models amid increasing safety concerns. This is analytically valuable for AI26 because evaluation, safety, competition, corporate governance and institutional authority are becoming articulated together rather than mapping cleanly onto fixed ideological camps.

For Phase 1, code claims and articulations rather than permanently labelling an organisation “x-risk”, “accelerationist” or “critical”. For Phase 3/4, this provides a potentially important temporal development to compare against earlier AI26 discourse.

### High signal: recent DNA work directly supports Phase 2 temporal analysis

Philip Leifeld's 2026 *Measuring Frame Evolution: Smoothed Temporal Framing Trajectories in Complex Policy Debates* extends discourse network analysis toward temporal frame trajectories and distinguishes patterns such as progressive/regressive and entangled/disentangled evolution.

**Why it matters:** this is unusually close to the planned Phase 2 bridge between discourse coalitions, time and network structure. It is a better candidate for Phase 2 methodological development than importing generic dynamic-network machinery first.

**Follow-up:** add it to the Phase 2 reading/method list and test whether LaclauGPT's actor–claim–concept–stance–time export can support comparable temporal trajectories without conflating “frame” with Laclaudian articulation.

### High signal: Haraway remains productive for the programme-level middle position

Sian Bayne's 2026 *Haraway's cyborg manifesto in education: after AI* explicitly re-evaluates the cyborg concept under contemporary AI conditions. Although education is not AI26's empirical domain, the paper is useful theoretical evidence that Haraway's boundary-crossing cyborg remains an active framework for thinking about human/technical relations after generative AI.

**Why it matters:** it supports the programme-level Haraway/cyborg/assemblage orientation that can investigate technological transformation without treating either acceleration or critique as the predetermined conclusion. This belongs primarily to Phase 2/programme framing. It does not justify importing PCM metaphysics or politics into Phase 1.

### Validation signal

Recent LLM-assisted qualitative-analysis work continues to support a human-in-the-loop architecture: staged extraction/coding can improve scale, but keyword-driven misclassification, granularity problems and the difference between agreement and interpretive quality make source-linked human adjudication essential. Phase 1 therefore needs validation of characteristic errors and evidence quality, not merely inter-rater-style agreement scores.

## Critical AI / cyborg position

The overall programme fits within Critical AI Studies through its attention to power, labour, ownership, inequality, infrastructure, governance and exclusion. Its distinctive contribution can remain a Haraway-style cyborg/assemblage openness to technological transformation: AI is neither assumed to be emancipatory through acceleration nor reduced in advance to domination. The empirical task is to analyse how these possibilities are constructed, institutionalised and contested.

Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude can remain nearby conceptual inspiration for this orientation. PCM-specific metaphysics and political theory stay outside locked Phase 1 unless the Phase 1 paper itself explicitly introduces them.

## Background noise / avoid

Do not add another database or graph backend unless a demonstrated interoperability or performance requirement demands it.
