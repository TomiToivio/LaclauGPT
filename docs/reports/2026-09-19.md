## Research watch

The strongest current AI26 empirical signal is that `safety`, `control`, `evaluation`, `competition`, `openness` and `pace` increasingly cross older ideological camps. Reuters reported a new Anthropic/Accenture commitment to independent frontier-model evaluation amid safety concerns. Recent reporting also shows simultaneous calls for coordinated slowing/oversight and opposition to coordinated slowdown from other industry actors. For AI26 this strengthens the decision to analyse claim-level articulations and political frontiers rather than treating organisations as permanently accelerationist, x-risk or critical-AI actors.

Keep distinct at minimum: catastrophic autonomy/loss-of-control; human-directed misuse/cyber/security; labour/extraction/inequality/infrastructure; governance/accountability; and acceleration/abundance/competitiveness. The research question is how these problem representations become articulated together, opposed or transformed in particular arenas.

For validation, recent 2026 qualitative-LLM work continues to support LaclauGPT's human-in-the-loop direction. Forster et al. describe a staged segmentation → coding → concept-development → quote-extraction pipeline with auditable provenance and expert validation. Pellegrini & Spinuzzi report that LLM coding can accelerate work but still produces keyword-driven misclassification and granularity errors that require critical human review. Liu et al.'s *Agreement Is Not Quality* remains especially important: human–LLM agreement alone is not a sufficient measure of interpretive quality, supporting blinded qualitative adjudication and error analysis alongside agreement metrics.

## Critical AI / cyborg position

Across the programme, Critical AI Studies remains the appropriate research home for analysing power, labour, ownership, inequality, infrastructure, governance and exclusion. The Haraway/cyborg/assemblage orientation should nevertheless keep technological transformation itself analytically open rather than treating either acceleration or critique as the predetermined answer. Panpsychic Cyborg Multitude can remain nearby conceptual inspiration for this orientation, but PCM-specific metaphysics/political theory should not enter Phase 1 unless `PAPER.md` itself introduces them.

## Tooling and architecture

No newly surfaced 4CAT, Zeeschuimer, DATS or local/open-LLM development currently warrants redesigning the architecture. Keep these as interoperable collection/analysis neighbours around LaclauGPT's canonical evidence/provenance contract. Local Ollama execution remains useful precisely because model/runtime versions can be recorded and compared; model equivalence should be tested rather than assumed.

The main repository also added GitHub Pages/research-discovery infrastructure. This improves public discoverability but is background to the scientific pipeline. It should not displace Phase 1 validation/integration work.
