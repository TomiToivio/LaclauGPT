## Correction — the `:8551` "second dashboard" was a phantom (issue #74, resolved 2026-10-10)

second dashboard: at most it was an **unmanaged orphan Streamlit process** — started
before the service unit and reparented to PID 1, so `systemctl restart` never touched
it — serving a possibly-stale copy of the same dashboard module. It is gone now, and
the visualization service exposes a single canonical port, `:8501`.

  "the visualization exposes `:8501` and `:8551`". That line is corrected to probe
  `:8501` only.

  signal: a process existing is not evidence of health.
