### New finding 1: the parliamentary arena cannot show the language stratification the paper promises

The new per-arena language cross-tab (#307, `corpus-audit --study-config`) is the sharpest
instrument yet on this. All three arenas declare their language scope; the **parliamentary arena
declares `[en, fi]` and holds 318 records of which 309 (97.2%) have no language label**. Of the
9 labelled rows, **8 are `en` and exactly 1 is `fi`**. The tool's own verdict applies verbatim:
"a stratified arena with 0 labelled records cannot demonstrate the stratification the study
promises."

Why it matters for Phase 1: section 4.2 requires the evaluation sample to be stratified by
arena, source type and **language**, and section 4.1 requires each arena to declare its language
scope. A declared `fi` parliamentary stratum supported by one labelled row cannot be sampled, so
either the label path is fixed for the Finnish governmental rows or the parliamentary arena's
language scope has to be stated as `en` with `fi` as an aspiration. This is evidence added to
**#294**, not a new issue: the same defect, now measured per arena.
