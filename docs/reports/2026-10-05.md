**Scientific publications (new to this report).**
- **arXiv:2605.12452, "The Algorithmic Caricature: Auditing LLM-Generated
  Political Discourse Across Crisis Events"** - constructs a paired
  1.79M-post corpus over nine crisis events and shows LLM-generated political
  discourse is *less dispersed* and more negative/abstract than observed
  discourse (a "Caricature Gap"). Relevant to LaclauGPT as a caution about
  using LLM-generated text as a stand-in for a speaker position, and as a
  population-level realism metric that could be borrowed for synthetic
  fixtures. *Credible (preprint, CSS/CL).*
- **arXiv:2606.28335, "LLM-Ideoplasticity"** - argues an LLM's political
  position is a *context-conditioned distribution*, not a fixed point, and
  measures the cohort's envelope as roughly one third the spread of major
  European parties. This is the strongest published argument yet *for* the
  paper's own stance that formations are provisional configurations and not
  labels to stamp on documents; it also strengthens the §4.2 "temperature 0
  is not determinism" line. *Credible (preprint, cs.CY).*
- **arXiv:2606.12922, "Polar"** - a 4,026-instance political-bias benchmark
  anchored in the Manifesto Project, U.S. and South Korean, evaluated across
  38 LLMs; finds bias varies by *presentation language* and political
  context. The language-of-presentation effect is directly relevant to the
  multilingual robustness check in §4.2 (en/fi source-language vs translated
  readings). *Credible (preprint; ARR 2026).*
- **arXiv:2606.12426, "Two Wrongs, No Right"** - audits social-desirability
  bias in LLM annotators for CSS across 72 cells, finding failures run in
  *both* directions (leniency on one model, overcorrection on others) and
  that **none of four prompting interventions fixes them**, with safety
  framing sometimes worsening stance distortion. Actionable and
  uncomfortable: it says the §4.2 "characteristic error" reporting must
  include direction, not just rate, and that prompt-level mitigation should
  not be assumed. *Credible (preprint, workshop).*
- **arXiv:2603.08406, "Sandpiper"** and **arXiv:2606.26203, "Agentic Analysis
  for Agentic Infrastructure"** - two LLM-annotation pipelines for
  discourse/governance corpora; the former's schema-constrained orchestration
  plus a human-label evaluation engine is close to the LaclauGPT design
  (codebook conformance + benchmark). Lower novelty for the paper; noted as
  related work only. *Credible (preprints).*
