## NooPunk #191 second pass: the citation audit (agent session, 09 Oct, later)

### 1. What triggered it

The seven-stream research pass on NooPunk #191 finished. Two of the seven streams delivered
**confident claims about the primary text itself** as corrections to the project's existing
citations. Both were verified against the live page at lawofone.info.

### 2. Both claims are FALSE

- **"`12.21` does not exist on lawofone.info."** It does — session 12 anchors **0–33 with no
  gaps**, and `12.21` *is* the Men in Black answer ("a thought-form type of entity … able to
  materialize and dematerialize"). The site's own `unified-index.php` lists it. **Root cause:
  an elided extract** — a summarising extractor dropped questions while keeping neighbouring
  anchors, which reads exactly like a numbering gap (the same copy was also "missing" 12.24).

  located in the octave, or eight[h] dimension, of the planet Saturn … in the rings."* The
  narrower true statement — non-third-dimensional, and the name is a translation artefact — is
  now the reskin's rule.

Either claim, taken on trust, would have put a **false statement about the source** into canon.

arrived from inside the research process, not from an outside gloss.

Grepped the whole canon for both false forms: **neither reached canon.** The only `12.21` and
`6.8` usages present are the correct ones (`rulebook/11_ONTOLOGY.md` §10.9 and
`RULEBOOK.md` §33.18 respectively). Nothing to repair.

(first-density servitors) — **agree** with the merged work: crystal technology is operator-gated
