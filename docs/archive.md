# Historical Research Archive

Two earlier repositories are preserved as **historical research artifacts**. They document the lineage of LaclauGPT but are **not part of the current software architecture**.

| Repository | Status | Historical role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LaclauGPT Multimodal Analysis](https://github.com/TomiToivio/LaclauGPT-Multimodal-Analysis) | Historical archive | Legacy EP2024 multimodal analysis pipeline and research documentation |
| [LaclauGPT TikTok Scraper](https://github.com/TomiToivio/LaclauGPT-TikTok-Scraper) | Historical archive | Legacy EP2024 TikTok collection tooling and research documentation |

For current development, use the four repositories listed on the [Project page]({{ '/project/' | relative_url }}).

Historical institutional research material remains subject to the governance, ethics, data-protection, licensing, and publication rules of the originating projects. See the repository's [legacy research policy](https://github.com/TomiToivio/LaclauGPT/blob/main/docs/LEGACY_RESEARCH.md).
